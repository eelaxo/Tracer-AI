Why we built this.

A wallet report shouldn't take an expert days to trace. $Tracer AI is our attempt to make that answer come back in minutes, not case-weeks.

The idea

Every fraud report starts pretty much the same way: one victim, one wallet address, and a trail that's already moving. By the time an investigator gets to it, the money may have already gone through burner wallets, a mixer, or even across to another chain. The one thing they actually need to know, which exchange it ended up at, is buried somewhere in all that movement.

Stratagem Crew built $Tracer AI to shortcut that: report a wallet, and get the exchange, the risk score, and a freeze-ready case file back, automatically.

What's working right now

  • A live backend that takes a wallet report, typed in or pulled from a simulated NCRP lookup
  • Hop-by-hop tracing through burner wallets, mixers and cross-chain bridges
  • A transparent risk score for every wallet, with the reasons shown, not hidden
  • Exchange attribution with a confidence score, plus a ready-to-send freeze request
  • A case history, so nothing traced is ever lost

What's next

  • Train the risk model on real labelled data instead of hand-set weights
  • Connect a real blockchain indexer, not a simulated graph
  • Real NCRP / SAHYOG integration
  • Move from a JSON file to a proper database
  • Host it publicly, not just on our own machines
